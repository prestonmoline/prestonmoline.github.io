**Short Review:**
This isn’t the greatest war movie you will watch, but it definitely gives you insight into D-Day from a perspective you’ve most likely never even knew about. For me, the acting was great, the story was interesting, and I enjoyed learning something new about D-Day, so I ended up having a good time watching this. The movie’s also not too long (1.5 hours pre credits), so it’s an easy movie to put on, watch, and enjoy.

**Rating:**
3.5/5
